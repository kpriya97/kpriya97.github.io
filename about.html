About Me

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Münster, working at the intersection of computational chemistry and machine learning for drug discovery.

Alongside my PhD, I work as an Application Scientist at BioSolveIT GmbH — a company specialising in computer-aided drug discovery software. I hold a Master of Science in Life Science Informatics from the University of Bonn.

My research interests include:

  • Molecular dynamics simulations of biomolecules
  • Structure-based drug design
  • Machine learning and deep learning applied to drug discovery

I am always open to collaboration on drug design and computational biology projects. Feel free to get in touch.
