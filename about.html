About Me

I am a Master's programme graduate in 'Life Science Informatics' at Bonn-Aachen Center for Information Technology, University of Bonn. I work as an Application Scientist at a company called BioSolveIT. At BioSolveIT, we make computer-aided drug discovery software. I am happy to conduct my research and help productize Molecular Mechanics/Molecular Dynamics calculations as an addition to our in-house existing software.

My research areas of interest mainly revolve around applying machine learning/ deep learning in drug discovery, molecular dynamics simulations and to develop relevant software tools and algorithms.

I am always open to collaboration on drug-design projects. I am also loooking for a PhD position to take the next step in my career. Feel free to write to me if any oppurtunities are open!
